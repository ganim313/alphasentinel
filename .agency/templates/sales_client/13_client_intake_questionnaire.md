# Template 13: Client Intake Questionnaire

**Purpose:** A form (often embedded on your website or sent via Typeform/Google Forms) to screen potential clients *before* you spend an hour on a discovery call with them. It weeds out low-budget or disorganized leads.

---

## 1. Company Information
- What is your company name and website?
- What is your role at the company? (Ensures you are talking to a decision-maker).

## 2. The Problem
- What is the primary business problem you are trying to solve with this software?
- Are you currently using an existing solution, spreadsheet, or legacy software? If yes, what is wrong with it?

## 3. The Solution
- What are the 3 "Must-Have" features for this project to be considered a success?
- Do you have any existing assets prepared? (Check all that apply)
  - [ ] Branding / Logos
  - [ ] Wireframes / UI Designs
  - [ ] Technical Specifications
  - [ ] Nothing, starting from scratch.

## 4. Timeline & Budget
- When do you ideally need this project launched?
  - [ ] ASAP (Within 4 weeks)
  - [ ] 1-3 Months
  - [ ] 3-6 Months
  - [ ] No strict deadline
- Custom software requires significant investment. To ensure we are aligned before our call, please indicate your approved budget range:
  - [ ] Under $5,000 (We do not take projects in this range)
  - [ ] $5,000 - $15,000
  - [ ] $15,000 - $50,000
  - [ ] $50,000+
